Terms of Service

GGGODLIN Labs operates PR Review Cloud. For questions about the service or these terms, contact philip@gggodlin.com.

Using the workspace

Use an account you control and upload only reports you have permission to share with the service. We handle basic GitHub sign-in identifiers, API key hashes and uploaded Markdown review reports. Reports are private by default. PR Review Cloud does not run LLMs on your behalf. See the privacy policy for how we use this data.

Pro monthly subscription

Pro costs US$19 per month. Polar is the Merchant of Record and handles payments and applicable taxes.

Cancel before your next renewal to avoid the next charge. You can use Pro through the end of the paid billing period. The current period is not refunded except where required by law or when Polar approves a refund.

Pro Pass and service retirement

The US$49 Pro Pass is a one-time purchase for an individual account with Pro access.

No monthly fee for as long as the service operates. We guarantee at least 12 months of operation from purchase. If we retire the service, we will give you at least 30 days' advance notice to export your reports. If we retire the service within 12 months of purchase, we will issue a pro-rata refund for unused months in that 12-month period.

See the refund policy for billing and refund requests.
