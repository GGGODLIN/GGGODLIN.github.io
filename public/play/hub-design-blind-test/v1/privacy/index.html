Privacy Policy

GGGODLIN Labs operates PR Review Cloud. Contact us at philip@gggodlin.com about your data or this policy.

Account and report data

We use basic account identifiers from GitHub sign-in to identify your workspace. We store API key hashes to authenticate CLI uploads and store the Markdown review reports you upload so you can view and compare them.

Reports are private by default. Anyone with a share link you create can view that report until you revoke the link. Review execution stays on your machine. PR Review Cloud does not run LLMs on your behalf.

Payments

Polar is the Merchant of Record. It processes payments and handles applicable taxes. Checkout and payment data are handled by Polar under its own privacy policy.

Data requests

Use the contact address above to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.
