# Checkpoint 09 · 2026-10-07

Stable combined equipment-and-character checkpoint for independent review.

## Usable character allocation

New characters have a coherent authored Lv20/9,193-EXP starting profile with six attributes at 14. The disclosed candidate ledger allocates 6,540 EXP and leaves 2,653 available. Players can raise or lower attributes in steps of 1, 10 or 100, see exact quoted costs/refunds, and watch derived stats update. The ledger conserves lifetime EXP and rejects unaffordable or invalid changes atomically. Combat blocks both allocation and refund.

The exponential allocation curve comes from the public Character Stats page. Integer rounding, zero normalization, symmetric refunds, min/max bounds and lifetime accounting are explicitly provisional/inferred, centralized in `ceil-cumulative-v1`, and linked from the interface. No EXP reward or level-up system is fabricated. See PROGRESSION.md.

## Existing players

Legacy characters keep their exact attributes, XP and remaining fixture points. They do not receive compensatory EXP or a silent rebalance. Tests include actual serialized states generated by frozen C07 source, including an active staff-plus-shield battle and a protected weapon.

## Equipment mechanics inherited from C08

Sourced two-hand eligibility, offhand removal without item deletion, first-equip level assignment, fixed assigned levels and over-level rejection. Legacy in-progress hand occupancy is preserved until that battle series ends.

## Browser usability

The resource strip remains visible when scrolling the battle interface. Equipment eligibility and attribute costs are visible before actions. No hidden enemy information or automatic combat is added.
