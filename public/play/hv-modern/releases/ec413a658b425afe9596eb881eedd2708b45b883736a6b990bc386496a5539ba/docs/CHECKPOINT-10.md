# Checkpoint 10 · 2026-10-07

## Playable earning → allocation → next challenge

First Blood, Learning Curves and Graduation are playable as bounded local Arena candidates. Full wave compositions are preserved: [1,1], [1,1,2,2], [2,2,2,2,3,3]. Entry checks level, preceding clear, UTC daily attempt and stamina. An attempt is spent on entry even if the character loses or flees. Crossing midnight preserves the entry-day accounting.

Won rounds award EXP using the sourced monster formula with explicit candidate ceil-at-round rounding. This implementation uses authored monster templates and PL100, Normal difficulty and inert external bonuses. Final victory awards the documented first/repeat Credits and a guaranteed item from the explicitly authored seven-template equipment pool. No original rarity distribution, hidden monster generation, tokens or full random loot are claimed.

The practice encounter remains separate and unrewarded. Legacy fixture characters remain unchanged and need an explicitly created EXP-ledger character for rewarded Arena; there is no invented conversion balance.

## Progression and persistence

Earned EXP enters the existing conserved allocation ledger, crosses candidate level thresholds and records future AP/Mastery entitlements without pretending the ability system is complete. Ordinary assigned equipment does not gain levels automatically. Items generated by the sample pool have unique IDs and preserve the source level-assignment restrictions.

Stamina uses an injected local clock, 1/hour regeneration, Great/Normal consumption and rewards, a 99 cap, and <2 entry rejection. This is not a server-authoritative clock or multiplayer anti-cheat system. RiddleMaster penalties are not invented.

## Usability

Activity cards display full waves, rewards, lock reasons and daily status. Entry confirmation explains the commitment. Arena results return to the next challenge; skill animation settings share one minimum input interval. The EXP meter reflects progress to the next level. Prior QA's attribute decrement/cannot-edit-in-combat wording is corrected.
