# Complete Better Cure candidate table

New work extending protected progression commit
`8028f3957474ef1151acef40864dcf9c7df5f2d3`. This completes only Better Cure's
community-documented table. It adds no new spell, proficiency growth or external
account benefit, and does not claim recovery of an earlier lost implementation.

## Evidence and bounded interpretation

On 2026-10-08, [Abilities / Supportive](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/Abilities#Supportive)
showed three ranks and a total investment of 10 AP. Its footer identified revision
64891. The inspected current body gives:

| Rank | Player level | Incremental AP | Total spent AP | Potency | Cooldown |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 70 | 4 |
| 2 | 35 | 3 | 5 | 85 | 3 |
| 3 | 65 | 5 | 10 | 100 | 2 |

Potency/cooldown are total effects for the assigned rank, not summed increases.
One Supportive slot activates the purchased ability. Sequential purchase remains
the existing candidate interpretation; no other prerequisite ability is documented.
Absence from the page is not proof of original-server behavior. No fourth rank is
listed, so purchase quotes now report a finite rank-three cap.

[Spells / Curative](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/Spells#Curative), footer revision65260,
lists Cure at level5/minimum proficiency0. Learning Better Cure at level1 does not
unlock the spell early. This pass did not establish unslotted baseline potency.

The immutable revision bodies/history were inaccessible and the official0.91
announcement returned403. The wiki itself warns some pages are outdated during
updates. These are community candidates for Persistent0.91, not verified original
server constants. Footer revision identifiers are not game versions.

## Healing, costs and conservation

Existing `CURATIVE_POLICY` and rational healing arithmetic are unchanged. The
executable Supportive proficiency remains fixed at0, so effective healing is
56%,68%,80% of baseHP before the final floor and current-HP cap. Final rounding
is still unverified. HP Tank increases maximum HP but never Cure's baseHP input.
Unassigned Cure retains the disclosed older authored baseline; see CURATIVE.md.

Rank3 adds5AP to the prior5AP investment. Ownership alone adds no healing or
cooldown benefit. Unslotting spends/refunds nothing. A permitted single reset
refunds exactly10AP for all three purchased ranks, leaves completed Training AP
and Credits unchanged, and consumes only the existing reset allowance. No paid
Training cost is refunded by ability reset. Active combat/wave pauses continue
to block learning, assignment and resets.

## Version and compatibility

Rules become `persistent-0.91-training-v23`; ability model becomes
`complete-cure-candidate-v1`. The full-Tank predecessor validator retains Tank
ranks0–10 and Cure0–2 and can carry all valid paid Boost AP. Migration clones only
the model identity, with no new rank, assignment, Credits, AP or reset allowance.
Older two-rank/three-key validators retain their own narrower limits.

Old active battles retain their prior policy tags and lower-rank Cure behavior.
A rank-three ownership claim in an old active policy is rejected, rather than
retroactively applying a new effect. Terminal historical battles may coexist with
new out-of-combat purchases. New battles pin the complete-Cure ability model.
Protected namespace, ownership lock, fresh reentry, stale import guards and
failed-save export behavior are unchanged.

## Verification contract

Run `npm run check`, `npm run build`, `npm run verify:checkpoint`.
The inherited suites remain enabled; obsolete rank-two-cap/UI expectations are
updated to the sourced third rank and migration expectations change only model
identity. Tests still compare full historical replay events/resources/RNG.
`protected-progression-cure-replay.json` was generated by the exact frozen prior
engine with an explicitly synthetic funded level65 Cure2/Tank3/paid-Boost1 profile;
it is not an original-server trace.

New tests cover typed rank boundaries, level64/65, incremental/cumulative AP,
assignment/reset conservation, real healing/cooldown and reload, previous-model
migration, old-active rejection, read-only tabs and failed saves. The integrity
gate preserves26 unaffected runtime modules byte-for-byte, including storage,
owner, Training and curative arithmetic. Abilities/data/engine integration and
app presentation are explicitly changed and covered by current release hashes.
Independent source and native browser acceptance remain separate gates.

The new UI interruption regressions also exposed an inherited ability-review
weakness under synthetic event invocation: dismissed or differently typed reviews
retained a pending purchase/reset.
This checkpoint clears reviews on dismissal/unrelated modals and binds each
confirmation to both its modal kind and unique review token. Late, substituted
or superseded confirmation events cannot commit a different review. The real-DOM
button dataset is reproduced in the UI harness; these guards do not replace
canonical save ownership and current-rank/AP revalidation.

Reproduction boundary: these tests manually invoke the delegated click listener
with fabricated/detached button targets. No delayed/debounced ability-confirm
callback was found, and no native-browser cancellation-related AP loss was
reproduced. Normal handlers are synchronous; the modal timer only moves focus.
The simple increasing review ID is defensive UI hardening, not a cryptographic
or malicious-script security boundary. Native browser behavior remains a separate
acceptance step.
